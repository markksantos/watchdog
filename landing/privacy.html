Privacy Policy

Watchdog for macOS · Last updated 23 July 2026

The short version: Watchdog runs entirely on your Mac. It has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and makes no network connections. Your captures are written to a folder on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see them, and we have no servers that could receive them.

Who we are

Watchdog is developed by Mark Studios LLC. If you have a question about this policy, contact us at hello@markksantos.com.

What Watchdog records

When you turn monitoring on, Watchdog uses your Mac's camera to look for faces or motion. When it detects something — or on a fixed interval, if you choose Always-On mode — it saves:

  • a still image (JPEG) from the camera;
  • on Watchdog Pro, an optional 5-second video clip (H.264, no audio — Watchdog never requests microphone access);
  • a small record for each capture: the time, which detection mode fired, the detector's confidence score, and the file name.

These images may include people other than you. Please read "Recording other people" below.

Where it is stored

Everything is written to your capture folder — ~/Pictures/Watchdog by default, or a folder you pick in Preferences. The files are ordinary images and videos on your own disk, stored unencrypted, readable by you and by anything else running under your macOS user account. Watchdog does not upload, back up, sync, or transmit them anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Watchdog collects no personal data, no usage data, and no diagnostics. It contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, does no tracking, and is not distributed with the network entitlement that would let it contact a server even if it wanted to.

If you buy Watchdog Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive only the anonymised sales reports Apple provides to every developer. We never see your name, your payment details, or your Apple Account.

How long captures are kept

  • Free: captures older than 3 days are permanently deleted, images and clips included. This happens automatically.
  • Pro: captures are kept until you delete them.

You can delete an individual capture from the gallery at any time, or erase everything at once with Preferences → Privacy → Delete All Captures. Deleting Watchdog does not remove your capture folder — delete it yourself if you want the images gone.

Camera permission

macOS asks for your permission before Watchdog can use the camera, and you can withdraw it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Whenever the camera is active, macOS shows a green indicator next to the menu bar. Watchdog cannot suppress that indicator and does not try to; the Watchdog menu bar icon also stays visible the entire time it is running.

Recording other people

Watchdog photographs whoever is in front of your Mac, including people who may not know the camera is running. Laws on recording other people vary considerably by country, state, and setting — workplaces, shared housing, and rented accommodation are often treated differently from a private home. You are responsible for using Watchdog lawfully where you are, and for informing people who may be recorded. If you are unsure whether a particular use is permitted, get advice before you rely on it.

Children

Watchdog is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to collect.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete personal data held about you. Because Watchdog stores everything locally and we hold no data about you, you exercise those rights directly: your captures are files on your own Mac, and you can view, copy, or delete them at any time. There is no account to close and no data for us to return.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Watchdog handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
